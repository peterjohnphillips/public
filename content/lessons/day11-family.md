## Notes

Nepali family terms are much more specific than English. Age and side of the
family both matter:

- Your father's younger brother is **काका**; his older brother is **ठूलो बुबा**.
- Your mother's brother is **मामा** (a completely different word).
- Older sibling vs younger sibling is never blurred: **दाइ / दिदी** vs
  **भाइ / बहिनी**.

For talking *about* family respectfully, use the honorific verb **हुनुहुन्छ**
instead of **छ**: *मेरो बुबा घरमा हुनुहुन्छ* ("my father is at home").

To say how many of something: number + counter. People take **जना**
(*तीन जना*, "three people"); things take **वटा** (*दुई वटा*, "two of them").

## Sentence Patterns

### मेरो ___ हुनुहुन्छ (Mero ___ hunuhunchha — My ___ is [respectful])
- मेरो बुबा शिक्षक हुनुहुन्छ | Mero buba shikshak hunuhunchha | My father is a teacher
- मेरो आमा घरमा हुनुहुन्छ | Mero aama gharma hunuhunchha | My mother is at home
- मेरो दिदी काठमाडौंमा हुनुहुन्छ | Mero didi Kathmandu ma hunuhunchha | My older sister is in Kathmandu

### मेरो परिवारमा ___ जना छन् (Mero pariwarma ___ jana chhan — There are ___ people in my family)
- मेरो परिवारमा चार जना छन् | Mero pariwarma char jana chhan | There are four people in my family
- मेरो परिवारमा छ जना छन् | Mero pariwarma chha jana chhan | There are six people in my family
- मेरो परिवारमा पाँच जना छन् | Mero pariwarma panch jana chhan | There are five people in my family

### तपाईंको ___ के गर्नुहुन्छ? (Tapaiko ___ ke garnuhunchha? — What does your ___ do?)
- तपाईंको बुबा के गर्नुहुन्छ? | Tapaiko buba ke garnuhunchha? | What does your father do?
- तपाईंको दाइ के गर्नुहुन्छ? | Tapaiko dai ke garnuhunchha? | What does your older brother do?
- तपाईंको श्रीमती के गर्नुहुन्छ? | Tapaiko shrimati ke garnuhunchha? | What does your wife do?

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Rita -->
तपाईंको परिवारमा कति जना हुनुहुन्छ?
Tapaiko pariwarma kati jana hunuhunchha?
How many people are in your family?

<!-- speaker: Sam -->
हामी चार जना छौं। बुबा, आमा, बहिनी र म।
Haami char jana chhaun. Buba, aama, bahini ra ma.
We are four. Father, mother, younger sister, and me.

<!-- speaker: Rita -->
तपाईंको बुबा के गर्नुहुन्छ?
Tapaiko buba ke garnuhunchha?
What does your father do?

<!-- speaker: Sam -->
उहाँ डाक्टर हुनुहुन्छ। आमा विद्यालयमा पढाउनुहुन्छ।
Uhan daktar hunuhunchha. Aama bidyalaya ma padhaunuhunchha.
He is a doctor. My mother teaches at a school.

<!-- speaker: Rita -->
तपाईंकी बहिनी कति वर्षकी हुनुभयो?
Tapaiki bahini kati barshaki hunubhayo?
How old is your younger sister?

<!-- speaker: Sam -->
उनी पन्ध्र वर्षकी भइन्। अझै विद्यार्थी छिन्।
Uni pandhra barshaki bhain. Ajhai bidyarthi chhin.
She is fifteen. She is still a student.
