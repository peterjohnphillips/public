## Notes

This lesson pulls together numbers, prices, and polite bargaining. Nepali counts
in a base-ten system but the words are irregular up to about 40, so drill 1–20
plus the tens (बीस, तीस, चालीस, पचास...) as raw memorisation.

Prices use **रुपैयाँ** (`rupaiyan`). "Per kilo" is **किलोको** — the `-को`
genitive doing "of / per". "How much for all of it" is **जम्मा कति?**

Bargaining phrases you'll actually use:

- **अलि महँगो भयो** — "that's a bit expensive"
- **अलि घटाउनुहोस् न** — "lower it a little, please"
- **अन्तिम कति?** — "what's your final price?"

`न` on the end of a request softens it, like "…won't you?".

## Sentence Patterns

### ___ कति पर्छ? (___ kati parchha? — How much is ___?)
- यो कति पर्छ? | Yo kati parchha? | How much is this?
- एक किलो आलु कति पर्छ? | Ek kilo aalu kati parchha? | How much is one kilo of potatoes?
- जम्मा कति पर्छ? | Jamma kati parchha? | How much is it altogether?

### मलाई ___ चाहियो (Malai ___ chahiyo — I would like ___)
- मलाई आधा किलो स्याउ चाहियो | Malai aadha kilo syau chahiyo | I would like half a kilo of apples
- मलाई एउटा झोला चाहियो | Malai euta jhola chahiyo | I would like a bag
- मलाई यो चाहियो | Malai yo chahiyo | I would like this one

### अलि ___ न (Ali ___ na — A little ___, please)
- अलि घटाउनुहोस् न | Ali ghataunuhos na | Lower it a little, please
- अलि छुट दिनुहोस् न | Ali chhut dinuhos na | Give a little discount, please
- अलि छिटो गर्नुहोस् न | Ali chhito garnuhos na | Hurry a little, please

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Grahak -->
दिदी, गोलभेडा कति पर्छ किलोको?
Didi, golbheda kati parchha kiloko?
Sister, how much are tomatoes per kilo?

<!-- speaker: Pasale -->
अस्सी रुपैयाँ किलो। एकदम ताजा छ।
Assi rupaiyan kilo. Ekdam taja chha.
Eighty rupees a kilo. They're very fresh.

<!-- speaker: Grahak -->
अलि महँगो भयो। साठी दिन्छु।
Ali mahango bhayo. Sathi dinchhu.
That's a bit expensive. I'll give sixty.

<!-- speaker: Pasale -->
सत्तरी दिनुहोस्। यो भन्दा तल जान मिल्दैन।
Sattari dinuhos. Yo bhanda tala jana mildaina.
Give seventy. I can't go lower than that.

<!-- speaker: Grahak -->
हुन्छ। एक किलो दिनुहोस्, अनि एउटा झोला पनि।
Hunchha. Ek kilo dinuhos, ani euta jhola pani.
Okay. Give me one kilo, and a bag too.

<!-- speaker: Pasale -->
लिनुहोस्। जम्मा सत्तरी रुपैयाँ भयो।
Linuhos. Jamma sattari rupaiyan bhayo.
Here you go. That's seventy rupees in total.
