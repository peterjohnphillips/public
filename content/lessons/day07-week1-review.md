## Notes

No new grammar today. This is a consolidation day: the dialogue below reuses
every pattern from Week 1 in one connected conversation — a first meeting between
a Nepali speaker and a foreign learner.

Work it in three passes:

1. Listen with all three lines visible.
2. Hide the English and shadow each line aloud.
3. Cover Ram's lines and try to produce them yourself before revealing.

The only new words are the connectives — **अनि, तर, किनभने, त्यसैले, पनि** —
which are what turn a list of sentences into actual speech. Drill those.

Week 1 self-check — you should be able to, without looking:

- Greet someone and give your name and country
- Ask and answer "how are you" and "what's your name"
- Ask where something is and how much it costs
- Say what you like, need, and want
- Say what you do in a normal day

## Sentence Patterns

### म ___बाट आएको हुँ (Ma ___bata aaeko hu — I am from ___)
- म अमेरिकाबाट आएको हुँ | Ma America bata aaeko hu | I am from America
- म अष्ट्रेलियाबाट आएको हुँ | Ma Australia bata aaeko hu | I am from Australia
- म बेलायतबाट आएको हुँ | Ma Belayat bata aaeko hu | I am from the UK

### मलाई ___ थाहा छ (Malai ___ thaha chha — I know ___)
- मलाई नेपाली थाहा छ | Malai nepali thaha chha | I know Nepali
- मलाई बाटो थाहा छ | Malai bato thaha chha | I know the way
- मलाई उनको नाम थाहा छ | Malai unko naam thaha chha | I know their name

### आज ___ (Aaja ___ — Today ___)
- आज मौसम राम्रो छ | Aaja mausam ramro chha | Today the weather is nice
- आज म व्यस्त छु | Aaja ma byasta chhu | Today I am busy
- आज मलाई खुसी लाग्यो | Aaja malai khusi lagyo | Today I feel happy

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Bimala -->
नमस्ते! तपाईंलाई भेटेर खुसी लाग्यो।
Namaste! Tapailai bhetera khusi lagyo.
Hello! Nice to meet you.

<!-- speaker: John -->
नमस्ते! मलाई पनि खुसी लाग्यो। मेरो नाम जोन हो।
Namaste! Malai pani khusi lagyo. Mero naam John ho.
Hello! Nice to meet you too. My name is John.

<!-- speaker: Bimala -->
तपाईं कहाँबाट आउनुभयो?
Tapai kahanbata aaunubhayo?
Where are you from?

<!-- speaker: John -->
म अमेरिकाबाट आएको हुँ। म यहाँ नेपाली सिक्दै छु।
Ma America bata aaeko hu. Ma yahan nepali sikdai chhu.
I am from America. I am learning Nepali here.

<!-- speaker: Bimala -->
धेरै राम्रो! तपाईंलाई नेपाली खाना मन पर्छ?
Dherai ramro! Tapailai nepali khana man parchha?
Very good! Do you like Nepali food?

<!-- speaker: John -->
हजुर, मलाई दाल भात धेरै मन पर्छ। तर पिरो खाना अलि गाह्रो हुन्छ।
Hajur, malai dal bhat dherai man parchha. Tara piro khana ali gahro hunchha.
Yes, I like dal bhat very much. But spicy food is a bit difficult.

<!-- speaker: Bimala -->
बिस्तारै बानी पर्छ। तपाईं कति दिन नेपालमा बस्नुहुन्छ?
Bistarai baani parchha. Tapai kati din Nepal ma basnuhunchha?
You'll get used to it slowly. How many days will you stay in Nepal?

<!-- speaker: John -->
तीन हप्ता। त्यसैले म हरेक दिन नेपाली पढ्छु।
Teen hapta. Tyasaile ma harek din nepali padhchhu.
Three weeks. So I study Nepali every day.

<!-- speaker: Bimala -->
राम्रो सोच! अब भेटौंला।
Ramro soch! Aba bhetaunla.
Good thinking! See you later.
