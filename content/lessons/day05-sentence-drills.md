## Notes

Day 4 gave you the "I am / I like / I need / I want" frames. Today is about
verbs and the **present habitual** tense — the one you use for "I eat rice",
"I go to work", "I get up early".

The pattern for regular verbs, first person:

- Take the dictionary form: **खानु** (`khanu`, "to eat")
- Drop `-नु`, add `-न्छु`: **खान्छु** (`khanchhu`, "I eat")

Polite second person swaps in `-नुहुन्छ`: **तपाईं खानुहुन्छ** (`tapai
khanuhunchha`, "you eat"). Third person is `-न्छ` / `-न्छिन्` (he / she).

Two things to drill hard:

1. **Word order.** Object before verb, always. "I drink tea" = *म चिया
   पिउँछु* (I tea drink).
2. **The `-न` infinitive** for "to be able to" and "want to": *म पढ्न सक्छु*
   (I read-can), *म जान चाहन्छु* (I go-want).

## Sentence Patterns

### म ___ (Ma ___ — I ___ every day)
- म भात खान्छु | Ma bhaat khanchhu | I eat rice
- म पानी पिउँछु | Ma paani piunchhu | I drink water
- म बिहान उठ्छु | Ma bihana uthchhu | I get up in the morning
- म घरमा बस्छु | Ma gharma baschhu | I live at home

### तपाईं ___? (Tapai ___? — Do you ___?)
- तपाईं नेपाली बोल्नुहुन्छ? | Tapai nepali bolnuhunchha? | Do you speak Nepali?
- तपाईं चिया पिउनुहुन्छ? | Tapai chiya piunuhunchha? | Do you drink tea?
- तपाईं कहाँ बस्नुहुन्छ? | Tapai kahan basnuhunchha? | Where do you live?

### म ___ सक्छु (Ma ___ sakchhu — I can ___)
- म नेपाली पढ्न सक्छु | Ma nepali padhna sakchhu | I can read Nepali
- म नेपाली बोल्न सक्छु | Ma nepali bolna sakchhu | I can speak Nepali
- म भात पकाउन सक्छु | Ma bhaat pakauna sakchhu | I can cook rice

### म ___ चाहन्छु (Ma ___ chahanchhu — I want to ___)
- म घर जान चाहन्छु | Ma ghar jana chahanchhu | I want to go home
- म नेपाली सिक्न चाहन्छु | Ma nepali sikna chahanchhu | I want to learn Nepali
- म आराम गर्न चाहन्छु | Ma aaram garna chahanchhu | I want to rest

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Anil -->
तपाईं बिहान कति बजे उठ्नुहुन्छ?
Tapai bihana kati baje uthnuhunchha?
What time do you get up in the morning?

<!-- speaker: Sita -->
म छ बजे उठ्छु। अनि चिया पिउँछु।
Ma chha baje uthchhu. Ani chiya piunchhu.
I get up at six. Then I drink tea.

<!-- speaker: Anil -->
त्यसपछि के गर्नुहुन्छ?
Tyaspachhi ke garnuhunchha?
What do you do after that?

<!-- speaker: Sita -->
म काम जान्छु। साँझमा घर आउँछु।
Ma kaam janchhu. Saanjhma ghar aaunchhu.
I go to work. In the evening I come home.

<!-- speaker: Anil -->
राति के गर्नुहुन्छ?
Raati ke garnuhunchha?
What do you do at night?

<!-- speaker: Sita -->
म खाना खान्छु र किताब पढ्छु। अनि सुत्छु।
Ma khana khanchhu ra kitab padhchhu. Ani sutchhu.
I eat food and read a book. Then I sleep.
