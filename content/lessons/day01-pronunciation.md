## Notes

Nepali has aspirated and unaspirated consonant pairs that English does not
distinguish. Getting these wrong changes the word, so drill them on day 1:

- **क ka** vs **ख kha** - the second has a puff of air after it.
- **त ta** vs **थ tha** - the tongue touches the teeth, not the ridge behind them.
- **ट ṭa** vs **त ta** - the retroflex ट curls the tongue back. This is the
  contrast English speakers find hardest.

Nepali is subject-object-verb. The verb lands at the end of the sentence, so
"I eat rice" comes out as "I rice eat".

## Vocab

- नमस्ते | namaste | hello / greetings | social
- धन्यवाद | dhanyabaad | thank you | social | alt: dhanyabad, dhanyawad
- हजुर | hajur | yes / pardon? (polite) | social
- म | ma | I / me | people
- तपाईं | tapai | you (polite) | people | alt: tapaai, tapain
- साथी | saathi | friend | people | alt: sathi
- नाम | naam | name | people | alt: nam
- राम्रो | ramro | good / nice | feelings
- ठिक | thik | fine / okay | feelings
- छ | chha | is / there is | verbs | Present tense of "to be" for things and states. alt: cha

## Sentence Patterns

### मेरो नाम ___ हो (Mero naam ___ ho — My name is ___)
- मेरो नाम राम हो | Mero naam Ram ho | My name is Ram
- मेरो नाम सुनिता हो | Mero naam Sunita ho | My name is Sunita
