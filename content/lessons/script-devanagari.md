## Notes

This is the source for the character recognition drill. Two or three short
bursts a day gets you sounding out words within a few days. Do not spend weeks
on handwriting.

Two things to hold onto:

- Every consonant carries an inherent **a** sound. क alone is "ka", not "k".
- A vowel written **after** a consonant is a matra, a modified mark rather than
  the standalone letter. क + ि = कि "ki". Standalone इ only appears at the start
  of a word.

The retroflex row (ट ठ ड ढ ण) is pronounced with the tongue curled back against
the roof of the mouth. English has no equivalent, and confusing it with the
dental row (त थ द ध न) is the single most common beginner error.
