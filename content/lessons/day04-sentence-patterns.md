## Notes

These are the highest-leverage templates in the whole course. Each one generates
hundreds of sentences once you can swap the slot freely. Drill the slot, not the
sentence: say ten different things with the same frame before moving on.

The `___` marker is what the fill-in-the-blank game reads, so keep it exactly
three underscores.

## Sentence Patterns

### म ___ हुँ (Ma ___ hu — I am ___)
- म विद्यार्थी हुँ | Ma bidyarthi hu | I am a student
- म अमेरिकी हुँ | Ma amerikee hu | I am American
- म साथी हुँ | Ma saathi hu | I am a friend

### मलाई ___ मन पर्छ (Malai ___ man parchha — I like ___)
- मलाई चिया मन पर्छ | Malai chiya man parchha | I like tea
- मलाई नेपाल मन पर्छ | Malai Nepal man parchha | I like Nepal
- मलाई भात मन पर्छ | Malai bhaat man parchha | I like rice

### मलाई ___ चाहिन्छ (Malai ___ chahinchha — I need ___)
- मलाई पानी चाहिन्छ | Malai paani chahinchha | I need water
- मलाई खाना चाहिन्छ | Malai khaana chahinchha | I need food

### म ___ गर्न चाहन्छु (Ma ___ garna chahanchhu — I want to ___)
- म काम गर्न चाहन्छु | Ma kaam garna chahanchhu | I want to work
- म कुरा गर्न चाहन्छु | Ma kura garna chahanchhu | I want to talk
