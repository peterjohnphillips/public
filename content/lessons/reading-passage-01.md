## Passage

काठमाडौं नेपालको राजधानी हो। यो धेरै ठूलो सहर हो।
Kathmandu Nepalko rajdhani ho. Yo dherai thulo sahar ho.
Kathmandu is the capital of Nepal. It is a very big city.

बिहान सडकमा धेरै मानिस हुन्छन्। कोही काम जान्छन्, कोही विद्यालय जान्छन्।
Bihaana sadakma dherai manis hunchhan. Kohi kaam janchhan, kohi bidyalaya janchhan.
In the morning there are many people on the road. Some go to work, some go to school.

चिया पसलमा मानिसहरू कुरा गर्छन्। हावा चिसो छ तर घाम राम्रो छ।
Chiya pasalma manisharu kura garchhan. Hawa chiso chha tara ghaam ramro chha.
People talk in the tea shops. The air is cold but the sun is nice.

साँझमा सहर शान्त हुन्छ। मलाई यो समय धेरै मन पर्छ।
Saanjhma sahar shanta hunchha. Malai yo samaya dherai man parchha.
In the evening the city becomes quiet. I like this time very much.
