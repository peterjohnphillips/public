## Notes

These are the phrases that keep you afloat before you can build sentences of
your own. Learn them as whole units — don't try to break them down grammatically
yet. The grammar comes in the Day 4 and Day 5 templates; right now the goal is to
be able to *say something* in any situation.

Politeness is not optional in Nepali. Almost every request takes the `-नुहोस्`
(`-nuhos`) ending, which is the polite imperative — `दिनुहोस्` "please give",
`भन्नुहोस्` "please say", `बोल्नुहोस्` "please speak". Drop it and you sound
rude, not casual.

Two survival reflexes to over-learn:

- **बुझिनँ** (`bujhina`) — "I didn't understand." Say it early and often.
- **बिस्तारै भन्नुहोस्** (`bistarai bhannuhos`) — "Please say it slowly."

`माफ गर्नुहोस्` opens almost any interaction with a stranger — it covers both
"excuse me" (to get attention) and "sorry" (to apologise).

## Sentence Patterns

### ___ कहाँ छ? (___ kahan chha? — Where is ___?)
- शौचालय कहाँ छ? | Shauchalaya kahan chha? | Where is the toilet?
- अस्पताल कहाँ छ? | Aspatal kahan chha? | Where is the hospital?
- पसल कहाँ छ? | Pasal kahan chha? | Where is the shop?
- बाटो कहाँ छ? | Bato kahan chha? | Where is the road?

### मलाई ___ चाहिन्छ (Malai ___ chahinchha — I need ___)
- मलाई सहयोग चाहिन्छ | Malai sahayog chahinchha | I need help
- मलाई पानी चाहिन्छ | Malai paani chahinchha | I need water
- मलाई पैसा चाहिन्छ | Malai paisa chahinchha | I need money
- मलाई डाक्टर चाहिन्छ | Malai daktar chahinchha | I need a doctor

### कृपया ___ (Kripaya ___ — Please ___)
- कृपया बिस्तारै भन्नुहोस् | Kripaya bistarai bhannuhos | Please say it slowly
- कृपया फेरि भन्नुहोस् | Kripaya pheri bhannuhos | Please say it again
- कृपया मलाई सहयोग गर्नुहोस् | Kripaya malai sahayog garnuhos | Please help me

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Tourist -->
माफ गर्नुहोस्, शौचालय कहाँ छ?
Maph garnuhos, shauchalaya kahan chha?
Excuse me, where is the toilet?

<!-- speaker: Local -->
त्यहाँ दायाँतिर छ।
Tyahan dayantira chha.
It is over there on the right.

<!-- speaker: Tourist -->
धेरै धन्यवाद। म नेपाली राम्रोसँग बोल्न सक्दिनँ।
Dherai dhanyabaad. Ma nepali ramrosanga bolna sakdina.
Thank you very much. I cannot speak Nepali well.

<!-- speaker: Local -->
केही छैन। बिस्तारै बोल्नुहोस्, म बुझ्छु।
Kehi chhaina. Bistarai bolnuhos, ma bujhchhu.
No problem. Speak slowly, I understand.

<!-- speaker: Tourist -->
हजुर। फेरि भेटौंला।
Hajur. Pheri bhetaunla.
Okay. See you again.
