## Notes

Nepali question words all start with **क** (`k`), which makes them easy to
group: के / को / कहाँ / कहिले / किन / कसरी / कति / कुन / कस्तो.

Two rules make questions much easier than they look:

1. **Word order does not change.** A statement becomes a question by swapping in
   a question word where the answer would go: *यो राम्रो छ* ("this is good") →
   *यो कस्तो छ?* ("how is this?").
2. **Yes/no questions** just add a rising tone, or the particle **नि** or a
   question mark in writing. *तपाईंलाई चिया मन पर्छ* → *तपाईंलाई चिया मन पर्छ?*

The verb still comes last, so the question word usually sits in the middle, not
the front: *तपाईंको नाम **के** हो?* not "*के तपाईंको नाम हो?*".

## Sentence Patterns

### ___ के हो? (___ ke ho? — What is ___?)
- यो के हो? | Yo ke ho? | What is this?
- त्यो के हो? | Tyo ke ho? | What is that?
- तपाईंको नाम के हो? | Tapaiko naam ke ho? | What is your name?

### तपाईंलाई ___ मन पर्छ? (Tapailai ___ man parchha? — Do you like ___?)
- तपाईंलाई नेपाल मन पर्छ? | Tapailai Nepal man parchha? | Do you like Nepal?
- तपाईंलाई नेपाली खाना मन पर्छ? | Tapailai nepali khana man parchha? | Do you like Nepali food?
- तपाईंलाई यो ठाउँ मन पर्छ? | Tapailai yo thaun man parchha? | Do you like this place?

### ___ कति पर्छ? (___ kati parchha? — How much does ___ cost?)
- यो कति पर्छ? | Yo kati parchha? | How much does this cost?
- चिया कति पर्छ? | Chiya kati parchha? | How much does tea cost?
- एउटा स्याउ कति पर्छ? | Euta syau kati parchha? | How much is one apple?

### तपाईं कहाँ ___? (Tapai kahan ___? — Where do you ___?)
- तपाईं कहाँ बस्नुहुन्छ? | Tapai kahan basnuhunchha? | Where do you live?
- तपाईं कहाँ जानुहुन्छ? | Tapai kahan januhunchha? | Where are you going?
- तपाईं कहाँ काम गर्नुहुन्छ? | Tapai kahan kaam garnuhunchha? | Where do you work?

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Grahak -->
नमस्ते दिदी। स्याउ कति पर्छ?
Namaste didi. Syau kati parchha?
Hello (elder sister). How much are the apples?

<!-- speaker: Pasale -->
एक किलोको दुई सय रुपैयाँ।
Ek kiloko dui saya rupaiyan.
Two hundred rupees per kilo.

<!-- speaker: Grahak -->
अलि महँगो भयो। दुई किलो दिनुहोस्।
Ali mahango bhayo. Dui kilo dinuhos.
That's a bit expensive. Give me two kilos.

<!-- speaker: Pasale -->
हुन्छ। अरू केही चाहियो?
Hunchha. Aru kehi chahiyo?
Okay. Do you need anything else?

<!-- speaker: Grahak -->
अहँ, त्यति भए पुग्छ। धन्यवाद।
Aha, tyati bhaye pugchha. Dhanyabaad.
No, that's enough. Thank you.
