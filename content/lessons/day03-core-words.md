## Notes

This is a pure vocabulary day. The words here are the ones that show up in
almost every sentence you will ever say: family members, everyday food, common
places, and the words for "today / tomorrow / yesterday / now". Learn them as
flashcards first, then come back and read the dialogue.

A few notes on the words:

- **आमा / बुबा** are "mother / father" as address terms too — you call your own
  parents this directly.
- Older and younger siblings have different words: **दाइ / दिदी** (older
  brother / sister) vs **भाइ / बहिनी** (younger). Nepali speakers also use
  दाइ / दिदी for slightly older strangers as a politeness.
- **भात** is cooked rice on the plate; **चामल** is uncooked rice in the sack.
  Different words, and mixing them up is a classic beginner tell.
- The verb still lands at the end: "I eat rice today" → *म आज भात खान्छु*
  (I today rice eat).

## Sentence Patterns

### यो ___ हो (Yo ___ ho — This is ___)
- यो मेरो घर हो | Yo mero ghar ho | This is my house
- यो मेरो परिवार हो | Yo mero pariwar ho | This is my family
- यो मेरो साथी हो | Yo mero saathi ho | This is my friend

### मसँग ___ छ (Masanga ___ chha — I have ___)
- मसँग पैसा छ | Masanga paisa chha | I have money
- मसँग समय छ | Masanga samaya chha | I have time
- मसँग एउटा प्रश्न छ | Masanga euta prashna chha | I have a question

### म आज ___ (Ma aaja ___ — Today I ___)
- म आज भात खान्छु | Ma aaja bhaat khanchhu | Today I eat rice
- म आज बजार जान्छु | Ma aaja bajar janchhu | Today I go to the market
- म आज घरमा बस्छु | Ma aaja gharma baschhu | Today I stay home

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Maya -->
यो मेरो परिवार हो। यो मेरी आमा हुनुहुन्छ।
Yo mero pariwar ho. Yo meri aama hunuhunchha.
This is my family. This is my mother.

<!-- speaker: Kiran -->
तपाईंको घरमा कति जना हुनुहुन्छ?
Tapaiko gharma kati jana hunuhunchha?
How many people are in your house?

<!-- speaker: Maya -->
हामी पाँच जना छौं। आमा, बुबा, दाइ, बहिनी र म।
Haami panch jana chhaun. Aama, buba, dai, bahini ra ma.
We are five people. Mother, father, older brother, younger sister, and me.

<!-- speaker: Kiran -->
राम्रो! मेरो परिवार सानो छ।
Ramro! Mero pariwar sano chha.
Nice! My family is small.
