## Notes

Directions in Nepali lean on a small set of position words plus the postposition
**-तिर** (`-tira`, "towards"): *दायाँतिर* "to the right", *बायाँतिर* "to the
left", *सिधा* "straight".

The key verbs:

- **जानु** (`janu`) "to go" — *सिधा जानुहोस्* "go straight"
- **मोड्नु** (`modnu`) "to turn" — *दायाँ मोड्नुहोस्* "turn right"
- **पुग्नु** (`pugnu`) "to arrive / reach" — *कति बेरमा पुगिन्छ?* "how long to
  get there?"

Landmarks take the postposition **-को छेउमा** ("next to") or **-को अगाडि** ("in
front of"): *मन्दिरको छेउमा* "next to the temple".

When someone gives you directions fast, fall back to Day 2: *बिस्तारै
भन्नुहोस्* and *फेरि भन्नुहोस्*.

## Sentence Patterns

### ___ कहाँ छ? (___ kahan chha? — Where is ___?)
- बस बिसौनी कहाँ छ? | Bas bisauni kahan chha? | Where is the bus stop?
- नजिकको अस्पताल कहाँ छ? | Najikko aspatal kahan chha? | Where is the nearest hospital?
- यो ठेगाना कहाँ छ? | Yo thegana kahan chha? | Where is this address?

### ___ मोड्नुहोस् (___ modnuhos — Turn ___)
- दायाँ मोड्नुहोस् | Dayan modnuhos | Turn right
- बायाँ मोड्नुहोस् | Bayan modnuhos | Turn left
- चोकमा बायाँ मोड्नुहोस् | Chokma bayan modnuhos | Turn left at the intersection

### ___ कति टाढा छ? (___ kati tadha chha? — How far is ___?)
- बजार कति टाढा छ? | Bajar kati tadha chha? | How far is the market?
- स्टेसन कति टाढा छ? | Steson kati tadha chha? | How far is the station?
- यहाँबाट कति टाढा छ? | Yahanbata kati tadha chha? | How far is it from here?

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Traveller -->
माफ गर्नुहोस्, ठमेल कतातिर पर्छ?
Maph garnuhos, Thamel katatira parchha?
Excuse me, which way is Thamel?

<!-- speaker: Passerby -->
सिधा जानुहोस्, अनि पहिलो चोकमा दायाँ मोड्नुहोस्।
Sidha januhos, ani pahilo chokma dayan modnuhos.
Go straight, then turn right at the first intersection.

<!-- speaker: Traveller -->
कति टाढा छ? म हिँडेर जान सक्छु?
Kati tadha chha? Ma hindera jana sakchhu?
How far is it? Can I walk there?

<!-- speaker: Passerby -->
नजिकै छ। दस मिनेटमा पुगिन्छ।
Najikai chha. Das minetma puginchha.
It's close. You'll get there in ten minutes.

<!-- speaker: Traveller -->
धेरै धन्यवाद। अलि बिस्तारै फेरि भन्नुहोस् है।
Dherai dhanyabaad. Ali bistarai pheri bhannuhos hai.
Thank you very much. Please say it again slowly.

<!-- speaker: Passerby -->
हुन्छ। सिधा, अनि पहिलो चोकमा दायाँ।
Hunchha. Sidha, ani pahilo chokma dayan.
Okay. Straight, then right at the first intersection.
