## Notes

This is the first dialogue long enough to drill as a response chain: read one
turn, then produce the next before revealing it. The two question forms here,
`के गर्दै हुनुहुन्छ?` and `कहाँ जाँदै हुनुहुन्छ?`, are worth over-learning.
They open more conversations than any other pair.

## Dialogue

<!-- speaker: Sunita -->
नमस्ते! के गर्दै हुनुहुन्छ?
Namaste! Ke gardai hunuhunchha?
Hello! What are you doing?

<!-- speaker: Ram -->
म चिया खाँदै छु। तपाईं कहाँ जाँदै हुनुहुन्छ?
Ma chiya khaandai chhu. Tapai kahaan jaandai hunuhunchha?
I am drinking tea. Where are you going?

<!-- speaker: Sunita -->
म बजार जाँदै छु। मलाई तरकारी किन्नु छ।
Ma bajaar jaandai chhu. Malai tarkari kinnu chha.
I am going to the market. I need to buy vegetables.

<!-- speaker: Ram -->
कति बज्यो?
Kati bajyo?
What time is it?

<!-- speaker: Sunita -->
दस बज्यो। मलाई ढिलो भयो।
Das bajyo. Malai dhilo bhayo.
It is ten o'clock. I am late.

<!-- speaker: Ram -->
ठिक छ, पछि भेटौंला।
Thik chha, pachhi bhetaunla.
All right, see you later.

## Sentence Patterns

### के गर्दै हुनुहुन्छ? (Ke gardai hunuhunchha? — What are you doing?)
- म काम गर्दै छु | Ma kaam gardai chhu | I am working
- म खाना खाँदै छु | Ma khaana khaandai chhu | I am eating food

### कहाँ जाँदै हुनुहुन्छ? (Kahaan jaandai hunuhunchha? — Where are you going?)
- म बजार जाँदै छु | Ma bajaar jaandai chhu | I am going to the market
- म घर जाँदै छु | Ma ghar jaandai chhu | I am going home
